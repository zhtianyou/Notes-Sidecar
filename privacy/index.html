Notes Sidecar

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Notes Sidecar is a macOS application provided by Zhang Tianyou. This policy explains how the application handles information.

What the app does

Notes Sidecar helps you work with content you choose from Apple Notes and shared content. You may connect an AI provider using your own account, API key, or supported sign-in method. We do not operate a developer-hosted relay server for your note content, audio, images, prompts, or AI responses.

Information processed locally

Depending on the features you use, Notes Sidecar may process locally on your Mac: text from an Apple Note you actively select or paste; images and audio you choose to share; locally generated image text and audio transcripts; prompts, drafts, conversations and AI responses; provider settings; and application preferences.

Automation is used only when you choose to connect the app to Apple Notes. It is intended to read the currently selected note and, only after confirmation, append an AI response. It is not designed to scan your Notes database in the background.

Local processing and privacy filtering

Images and supported audio are analyzed on your Mac when available. Original image and audio files are not sent by Notes Sidecar to our servers. Before note-derived text is eligible to be sent to an AI provider, the app applies a local privacy filter intended to identify and exclude sensitive passages. No automated filter is perfect; review what you choose to send.

AI providers

When you request an answer, permitted context is sent directly from your Mac to the AI provider you selected. That provider may receive request content, your IP address, and authentication credentials. Each provider has its own privacy policy, terms, retention practices, pricing, availability, and security controls. You are responsible for reviewing them and selecting an appropriate provider.

Credentials and storage

API keys, OAuth access tokens, and refresh tokens are stored locally in macOS Keychain. Local conversations and drafts are stored in the app's Application Support storage. The Share Extension exchanges data with the main app through a local App Group container.

What we collect

We do not operate a Notes Sidecar account system, cloud sync service, analytics service, advertising service, or developer-hosted content relay. We therefore do not receive your note content, images, audio, prompts, AI responses, or provider credentials through such a service.

Your choices and contact

You may decline or revoke macOS Automation and Accessibility permissions. Accessibility is optional and core chat can still be used without it. For privacy questions, contact tianyou.zhang@gmail.com.
